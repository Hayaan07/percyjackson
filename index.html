What is Percy Jackson?

Percy Jackson & the Olympians is a fantasy adventure series by Rick Riordan, in which the gods of ancient Greece are real, alive and still living in the modern world. They move with the centre of Western civilization, and right now that is America, with Mount Olympus floating above the Empire State Building.

The gods still have children with mortals. These half-blood children are called demigods, and they are monster magnets. Most of them go to Camp Half-Blood on Long Island to train and stay safe.

The story

Percy Jackson is a twelve-year-old with ADHD and dyslexia who keeps getting kicked out of schools. He discovers that his father is Poseidon, god of the sea, and he is then accused of stealing Zeus's master lightning bolt. With his friends Annabeth, daughter of Athena, and Grover, a satyr, Percy crosses America to find the real thief and avert a war between the gods.

His quest takes him past the Underworld, the Sea of Monsters and the Labyrinth, and ends in a final war against Kronos, the Titan Lord of Time, who rises to destroy Olympus.

Greeks and Romans

The gods have two forms, Greek and Roman. Greek demigods train at Camp Half-Blood, while Roman demigods live at Camp Jupiter in California. In The Heroes of Olympus, the two camps meet for the first time, and seven heroes from both sides must unite to stop Gaea, the Earth Mother, from rising.
